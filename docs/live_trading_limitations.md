# Live Trading Limitations

This repository is a research and decision-support framework, not a live trading bot.

The HMM remains the core regime model. Neural networks, forecasts, observable Markov models,
volatility forecasts, options features, research-public features, and alt-data overlays are
confirmation, benchmark, risk sizing, or diagnostic layers.

Paper trading is simulation-only. No real broker APIs are called, and no output should be treated
as investment advice or a profitability guarantee.
